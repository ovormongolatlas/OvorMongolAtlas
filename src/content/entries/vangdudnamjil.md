**STUB entry — VERIFY-GATE.** Vangdudnamjil is documented in this atlas's master chronology only as [Gungsangnorbu](/entry/gungsangnorbu)'s immediate predecessor as jasag of Kharachin Right Banner, Josotu League, a succession dated to 1898 — itself a reference-tier date, not independently citable scholarship. No other fact about him (birth, death, tenure length, family, or any activity as jasag) is available to this atlas from any source currently in hand. The single frontmatter year above (1898) marks the one documented point — the succession — not a birth, death, or tenure span.

**VERIFY-GATE (coverage now confirmed; source not yet consulted):** the open question in the previous version, whether Jagchid's Gungsangnorbu study actually treats this predecessor, is now answered: it does. The index of Jagchid's *Prince Gungsangnorbu and Inner Mongolian Modernization* lists "Prince Wangdudnamjil" (also romanized Wangdut Namzil) as an index term, so the same acquisition that unblocks the [Gungsangnorbu](/entry/gungsangnorbu) entry (Jagchid, "Prince Gungsangnorbu: Forerunner of Inner Mongolian Modernization," *Zentralasiatische Studien* (1979), pp. 147–158; reprinted as Chapter 16 of *Essays in Mongolian Studies*) is the source to consult for this figure. This atlas does not yet hold that study. Do not expand this entry until it has been consulted directly, and treat whatever it yields on Vangdudnamjil as likely brief.
