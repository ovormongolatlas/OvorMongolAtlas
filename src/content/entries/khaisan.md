Khaisan, in full Bayantömöriin Khaisan, was an official of the Kharchin banner of Josotu League ruled by Prince [Gungsangnorbu](/entry/gungsangnorbu), and one of the Inner Mongols who took an active part in Mongolia’s independence movement of 1911 [1]. He first came to Khüree (Urga) in 1907 and was active in the politics of the new Mongolian state from 1910 to 1915 [1]. His birth and death are not recorded in the sources consulted; the years above run from his arrival in Khüree to the end of his recorded activity.

*A note on sources.* Most of what is known here comes from a Bogd Khan decree of 1913 in Mongolia’s National Central Archives, transcribed and introduced by S. Shijirbaatar on a teachers’ history blog [1]. The transcription has not been checked against the original. Tachibana Makoto’s archive-based study [2] and Baabar’s essay [3] place him in the 1913 campaign.

## Rewarded by the Bogd Khan (1912–1913)

In the spring of 1912, the decree says, Khaisan worked with particular zeal and devotion in founding the new state. The Bogd Khan granted him the hereditary rank of *ulsad tuslagch gün* (duke who aids the state), the title *Chin Zütgelt* (“truly devoted”), a seal, and a post as deputy official of the Interior Ministry, along with land of his choosing for his household [1]. Shijirbaatar identifies that land as the six guard posts around Büür in Selenge [1]. The decree adds that Khaisan was later sent west on the work of pacifying the western frontier, and that he was raised to the rank of *tüshee gün* for that service. The written patent was issued in the third year of the Bogd Khan’s reign (1913) [1]. Writers on the period, Shijirbaatar notes, describe him as close to the Bogd Khan and to the head of the Interior Ministry, Tserenchimed [1].

## The Five Roads campaign (1913)

On 25 January 1913 the Mongolian war ministry assigned Khaisan and [Babujab](/entry/babujab) to the Dariganga route of the army it was sending into Inner Mongolia [2]. Baabar names the same pair on that route [3]. The [Five Roads campaign](/entry/five-roads-campaign) entry follows the war.
